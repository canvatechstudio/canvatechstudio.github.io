Privacy Policy

How MotionCanvas Handles Your Data

Your privacy is important. MotionCanvas stores your projects, settings, photos, videos, and audio locally on your device. We do not upload your project files, photos, videos, or audio recordings to our own servers. You may be asked to provide access to your photo library, camera, microphone, or audio files so the app can create stop motion projects, add media to a project, trigger the camera shutter, record audio, or save exported videos/photos to your library.

We do not access your project content unless you choose to export or share it using features provided by iOS.

Advertising

Free users may see ads in the app. We use Google AdMob and may use AdMob mediation or bidding partners, such as BidMachine, DT Exchange, and other ad sources configured in AdMob, to request and display ads.

These advertising services may collect or receive information such as your device type, app information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising identifier where permitted, ad request information, ad interaction information, and privacy or consent signals. This information may be used to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted by your choices and applicable law, personalize ads.

We do not share your photos, videos, audio, or project files with advertising partners.

Privacy Choices and Tracking

Where required, MotionCanvas uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for and manage your privacy choices before requesting ads. Depending on your region, you may be able to choose whether ads are personalized and may be able to update those choices from the app settings.

On iOS, you may also be asked for permission to allow tracking. If you deny tracking permission or limit ad tracking in your device settings, we respect that choice and the advertising services receive the limited signals made available by iOS and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and diagnostic reports that help us improve app stability, identify issues, and enhance the user experience. Crash reports may include technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, session information, and crash logs. We use this information only to diagnose and fix app problems.

Access to crash report data is restricted to authorized personnel.
